About Baltimore Sound Guy

A lifelong music and tech nerd who loves helping Baltimore throw a great event.

I’ve always loved two things: music and technology. Baltimore Sound Guy is what happens when you put them together and add a love for the community.

  1. The mixtape days

    In middle and high school I discovered Napster and started selling custom mix CDs to friends. Picking the right song for the right moment has been my thing ever since.

  2. Behind the decks

    In college my interest in electronic music grew, and I started DJing. These days my sets are mostly Top 40 and crowd favorites, the songs that get everyone on the dance floor, mixed to fit your crowd and the vibe of your event.

  3. Running the stage

    About five years ago, a local community festival was in a bind and desperately needed someone to run stage sound. I stepped up, learned how, and have been doing it ever since.

  4. Today

    I work with several local non-profits to help them run successful events, and I bring the same care to every party, meeting, and movie night I work.

What That Means for Your Event

Music First

From mix CDs to Top 40 dance floors, reading the room and picking the right track is second nature.

Tech That Works

Community Minded
