What we bring

Services

Sound, video, and tech support for parties, community events, and meetings across Baltimore and the surrounding area.

DJ & Karaoke

Keep the energy up with a DJ who reads the room, or hand the mic to your guests with a full karaoke setup.

  • DJ sets tailored to your crowd and event
  • Karaoke with microphones, song selection, and screen
  • Announcements and background music between activities

Great for: birthday and house parties, fundraisers, school events, bar and community nights.

Event Audio Rentals

Need sound but not a DJ? Rent the gear, and get help choosing what fits your space and crowd.

  • PA speakers
  • Mixers
  • Wired and wireless microphones
  • Speaker and mic stands, cables, and accessories

Great for: speeches, ceremonies, block parties, live performances, and outdoor gatherings.

Hybrid & Virtual Meetings

Make sure the people on Zoom or Teams can see and hear as well as the people in the room.

  • Cameras, microphones, and speakers for hybrid rooms
  • Setup and testing before your meeting starts
  • On-site technical support during the event

Great for: conferences, board meetings, trainings, panels, and town halls.

Projectors & Screens

Big-screen fun indoors or out, with sound to match.

  • Projector rentals
  • Indoor and outdoor screens
  • Audio to pair with your movie or presentation

Great for: backyard movie nights, watch parties, school and church events, presentations.

AV Repair & Consultation

Gear acting up, or not sure what to buy? Get practical help from someone who knows AV.

  • Equipment troubleshooting
  • Upgrades and system setup
  • Advice on what to buy (and what you don’t need)

Great for: churches, schools, small venues, offices, and home setups.

Not sure what you need? Tell us about your event and we’ll figure it out together.
